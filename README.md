# 📊 Vrinda Store – Annual Sales Report 2022

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project analyzes **Vrinda Store’s sales performance for the year 2022**.  
The main goal is to help the store **understand customer buying behavior**, identify key sales trends, and make **data-driven decisions** to increase growth in 2023.

---

## 🎯 Objective  
**"To create a detailed annual sales report for Vrinda Store (2022) that reveals customer insights, improves marketing strategies, and boosts sales performance for the upcoming year."**

---

## 📁 Dataset  
The dataset includes customer and order-related information such as:  
- Customer demographics  
- Order details  
- Shipping information  
- Product categories  
- Payment modes  

---

## 🛠 Tools Used  
- **Microsoft Excel**  
- Pivot Tables  
- Charts & Visualizations  
- Data Cleaning & Filtering  
- Sales Dashboard

---

## 📈 Key Analysis Performed  
- Month-wise sales performance  
- Top-performing states & regions  
- Best-selling product categories  
- Gender-based customer analysis  
- Order channel contribution (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.)  
- Overall profit & quantity trends  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
✔ **Women** are more likely to purchase than men  
✔ Most sales come from **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh**  
✔ **Adult age group (30–49)** contributes the highest sales  
✔ **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra** drive maximum orders  
✔ Festival seasons show peak sales months  

---

## 📊 Final Dashboard  
A dynamic Excel dashboard was created to visualize all insights clearly and attractively.
